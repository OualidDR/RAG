# Nova Coffee Co. Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off
Every full-time employee receives 18 days of paid vacation per year. After three years of service this increases to 22 days.
Sick leave is separate: 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required only if you are absent for more than 3 consecutive days.

## Salary and Tips
Salaries are paid on the 28th of each month by bank transfer.
All tips are pooled and shared equally among the staff on shift.
Employees get a 30% discount on all drinks and food.

## Safety
The fire extinguisher is located behind the counter next to the milk fridge. The first aid kit is in the back office.
